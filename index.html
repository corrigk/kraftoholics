FAQ & shipping

The questions we get most — reach out in a message if yours isn't here.

Are the bracelets really waterproof?

Yes — the wax rope is sealed and knotted to hold up through swimming, showers and everyday sweat. It's meant to be worn every day, not just on special occasions.

Can I get a bracelet in my school's colors?

Absolutely. Pick from the rope color and charm options on any bracelet's product page, or send a message with your school colors and mascot for a fully custom set.

How does a custom order work?

Message us through the chat bubble or contact form with the colors, charm and any personalization (like a name or initial) you'd like. Most custom pieces are confirmed within a day and made to order.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders are made, wrapped and shipped within 3–5 days. Fully custom pieces may take a little longer — you'll be messaged with a timeline before it ships.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Ready-made pieces can be exchanged within 14 days if unworn. Because custom and personalized pieces are made just for you, those are final sale — reach out if something arrives damaged.

How do I care for a watercolor print?

Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from moisture, and it'll hold its color for years. Framing behind glass is the best way to keep it looking fresh.
